📋 Server Rules & Guidelines

Community standards and acceptable use policies

⚖️ Community Rules

🎮 General Gameplay

  • Respect all players and maintain a positive community environment
  • No harassment, discrimination, or toxic behavior
  • Use appropriate language in public channels
  • No real money trading (RMT) of in-game items or currency
  • Report bugs and exploits instead of abusing them

🤖 Third-Party Tools

  • Windower and Ashita clients are permitted
  • Quality of life addons are generally allowed
  • Automation tools (bots) are strictly prohibited
  • Speed hacks and movement exploits are banned
  • When in doubt, ask staff about specific addons

💰 Economic Policies

  • No price manipulation or market cornering
  • Fair pricing encouraged for community health
  • Gil selling/buying for real money is prohibited
  • Report suspicious economic activity to staff

🚨 Enforcement

📝 Reporting Issues

Use the /report command in-game or contact staff through Discord for serious violations.

⚠️ Warning System

  • Warning: First offense for minor violations
  • Suspension: Temporary ban (1-30 days)
  • Permanent Ban: Severe or repeated violations

📞 Appeals Process

Appeals can be submitted through Discord. Include your character name, the reason for punishment, and why you believe it should be overturned.
